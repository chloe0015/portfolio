DeepBrain

Shaping a K–12 brain-training platform with an AI study buddy

deepbraintechnology.com/en↗
Open site ↗

DeepBrain is a brain-training platform for K–12 students. Students build memory, logic and focus through brain games, with Wukoo, an AI study buddy, by their side.

I owned the visual layer of the live site. Spacing and type varied from page to page, so nothing guided the eye. I set consistent spacing and a clear type hierarchy that every page now follows. I also designed the student dashboard and built animations in code.
